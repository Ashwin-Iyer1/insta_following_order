Evidence, with its limits.

Source position means position in a file, not a verified follow date. Missing dates stay missing. Comparisons match usernames case-insensitively; a renamed account can appear on both sides of a difference.

Official JSON exports with relationships_following and the original exporter’s {username, full_name} arrays are supported. Completeness is shown when explicitly recorded; it cannot be inferred from a list alone.
